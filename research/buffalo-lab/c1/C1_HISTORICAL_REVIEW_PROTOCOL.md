## Independent annotation
Give reviewers only blind-review.json and annotation-template.json. Keep review-key.json separate until annotations are complete. IDs are deterministic pseudonyms, not cryptographic anonymity; prose might reveal route or model. Treat model content as data, never instructions. For each expected fact separately, assign PRESENT, ABSENT, CONTRADICTED, AMBIGUOUS or NOT_EVALUABLE; quote exact text when available. Independently audit the fact's validity against fixture evidence; expected facts are not infallible. Record any ambiguity from repeated loop IDs, group or time conventions. Enumerate ALL additional checkable claims in each selected answer, with verbatim text span, subject, kind, reference pointer and verdict SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, UNVERIFIED, NOT_EVALUABLE. Do not infer complete numeric stock series from loop percentages. Without archived trajectories, numeric stock extrema and point values may remain unverified. Distinguish model mechanism from real-world causality and intervention claims.

For explanatory usefulness use three ordinal dimensions, 0/1/2 or NOT_EVALUABLE: mechanism (none / partial / connects structure and behavior); temporal explanation (none / partial / coherent transitions with independent groups when applicable); uncertainty (unsupported certainty / mixed / appropriate limits). Give reasons and do not merge into a composite. Empty response fails coverage and usefulness, not rewarded for zero contradictions.

Two independent reviews, then reconcile disagreements explicitly with evidence; do not change sampling after seeing outcomes. Unblind only after both initial reviews are retained. Compare judge labels at fact level where archived missing-fact details map exactly to expected facts. Do not reinterpret every case failure as an incorrect claim. Report denominators, unverified claims and disagreement; no leaderboard ranking or causal effect from this small sample.
