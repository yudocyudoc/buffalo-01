## A5 — system change (protocol before execution)

Source: Schoenberg, Eberlein, Davidsen, *Measuring the change in behavior of a system with a single metric*, SDR 40:e1754 (2024), https://doi.org/10.1002/sdr.1754, eqs 4–6. For each stock, sum absolute instantaneous net flow over the complete uniform observation grid; divide the absolute net flow at each time by that sum; add across stocks and normalize by the peak over the horizon. Do not substitute gross throughput, absolute stock levels, raw loop scores, or saved-step differences. Zero-change stock contributes zero and all-static series returns zero: explicit defensive convention for zero denominators. No threshold for ignoring small nonzero stock changes. This is an independent postprocessor of native flow trajectories, not a claim of a native Simlin system_change API. Inspected run.py/sim.py/analysis.py at upstream candidate 3ac0a43f096b2400ec3a03740a8ea72854583e12 without finding such API; not proof of its absence from every version or the wheel source. Four native observations: material flow model with opposing net flows and static throughput, unit-rescaled variant, and A4 coupled Euler/RK4 full grids. Compare complete instantaneous flow series and metric against independent expectations; adjacent stock differences are diagnostic only. Metric tolerance 1e-9, no scientific admission. Seven controls: manual arithmetic, scale/sign, all static, horizon dependence, net versus throughput, reject incomplete inputs, and stock-duplication non-invariance. The metric depends on model boundary and observation horizon, not an absolute system-wide change measure; it does not rank causal interventions. Raw loop weighting by system change not implemented as a new published LTM score. Native observations pending. No production changes.
